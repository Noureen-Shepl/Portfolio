01 / MY STORY

About Me

It started with a simple question:
“How does this actually work?”

My interest in technology didn't start when I entered university. I've been curious about technology and the way things work since I was young. I was always interested in exploring what's behind the systems, devices and digital experiences we use every day.

Over time, I realized that I didn't only enjoy using technology — I enjoyed understanding it. I naturally found myself analyzing things around me, breaking problems into smaller pieces, looking for patterns and asking why something works the way it does.

That curiosity gradually became a real interest in Artificial Intelligence, data and programming. What fascinated me most about AI was the idea that technology could learn from information, recognize patterns and help solve problems in intelligent ways.

Today, I'm studying Artificial Intelligence Engineering at Tanta University, where I'm building my foundation in Python, problem solving, programming, digital logic and AI concepts. At the same time, I keep learning outside the classroom through technical courses, programming challenges and hands-on projects.

I'm especially interested in the connection between AI and Data Analysis because it combines two things I genuinely enjoy: understanding information and using technology to turn that understanding into something useful.

For me, this portfolio isn't just a collection of projects. It's a record of a journey I'm still building — one problem, one dataset and one new idea at a time.
